POLICY AGAINST VIOLENCE AND HARASSMENT AT WORK

version of 1.05.2024

1. Subject Matter

  1. The company under the name THEMELI S.A. observes all measures and obligations concerning the implementation of the provisions of Part II of Law 4808/20211 on the prevention and combating of all forms of violence and harassment, including gender-based violence and harassment and sexual harassment.
  2. The purpose of this policy is to create and consolidate a working environment that respects, promotes and safeguards human dignity and the right of every person to a world of work free from violence and harassment. THEMELI S.A. declares that it recognises and respects the right of every employee to a working environment free from violence and harassment and that it does not tolerate any such conduct, of any form, by any person.
  3. This policy is adopted in accordance with Articles 92 and 103 of Law 4808/2021 and the regulatory legislation issued pursuant to them, and covers the persons referred to in paragraph 14 of Article 3 of Law 4808/2021.

2. Scope of Application

  1. This policy applies to and concerns all employees and workers of THEMELI S.A., irrespective of their contractual status, including those engaged under a contract for work, independent services or salaried mandate, those engaged through third-party service providers, as well as persons undergoing training, including trainees and apprentices, volunteers, and workers whose employment relationship has ended.
  2. Forms of conduct constituting violence and harassment against any of the above persons may take place in particular:
    • in the workplace, including public and private spaces and places where the employee performs work, receives remuneration, takes a break, etc., and
    • during commuting to and from work, other journeys, trips and training, as well as at events and social activities related to work

3. Definitions of Violence and Harassment

  1. In accordance also with the European Agency for Safety and Health at Work, violence at work means any incident in which a person is insulted, threatened or attacked in the course of work, in circumstances which put at risk their safety, health or work performance. It covers insults, threats, and physical or psychological attacks, exerted by persons inside and outside the Organisation against a person in the workplace. Violence may also have a racial or sexual dimension.
  2. Harassment in the workplace is defined as repeated, unjustified conduct towards an employee or a group of employees, posing a risk to their health and safety. Examples include unfavourable treatment, humiliation, undermining, etc. Harassment may consist of aggressive conduct, both verbal and physical, as well as less visible systematic actions such as the devaluing of an employee’s work or their social isolation in the workplace. Harassment often involves an element of unfair use or abuse of power, against which victims may be unable to protect themselves. Instructions concerning work, or guidance to an employee aimed at improving their efficiency and consequently at the smooth and more efficient operation of the company, are not considered harassment.

Sexual harassment means any form of unwanted verbal, psychological or physical conduct of a sexual nature, which aims at or results in the entering into a sexual relationship through the creation of an intimidating, hostile, degrading, humiliating or offensive environment, or through the provision of work-related benefits. The expression of liking, a courteous expression of desire for romantic or sexual contact, the recognition of abilities and, in general, conduct expressing interest that does not offend the dignity of persons are not considered harassment, and specifically sexual harassment.

4. Assessment of the risks of violence and harassment at work

Indicatively, conduct relating to violence and harassment of any kind in the workplace includes:

  • threats of any kind
  • demeaning, offensive or humiliating conduct
  • cyberbullying
  • physical violence and harassment by gestures (touching, pinching, pushing)
  • retaliation or intimidation for reporting or threatening to report
  • sexual harassment
  • offering benefits (e.g. career advancement) in exchange for a sexual act
  • spreading malicious comments or insulting someone on discriminatory grounds (for example, on grounds of age, gender, any disability, sexual preference, etc.)
  • sexual innuendo, etc.
  • Comments about physical appearance, gender, religion, race or sexual orientation that cause shame or embarrassment.
  • Use of obscene, threatening or offensive language
  • Exploitation of a position of power within the Company to degrade the employee’s personality or to propose entering into a sexual relationship.

The above examples are indicative and not exhaustive of the prohibited acts falling within the definitions of violence and harassment.

5. Measures for preventing, controlling, limiting and addressing the risks of violence and harassment at work and for monitoring such incidents or forms of conduct

To prevent, control, limit and address the risks of violence and harassment at work and to monitor such incidents or forms of conduct, the following measures have been taken:

  • Ensuring policies and procedures that address violence and harassment, including prevention measures, reporting procedures and sanctions for violations.
  • Informing employees about the risks of violence and harassment, their effects and the reporting procedures.
  • Encouraging personnel to complain about and report such incidents in accordance with the complaints procedure.
  • Providing support to victims of violence and harassment, or to victims of domestic violence, including psychological support and guidance on the procedures for reporting and seeking justice, as well as on their reintegration into the workplace
  • Providing all employees with access to this policy and the related procedures
  • Implementing monitoring mechanisms for detecting possible incidents of violence and harassment, as well as for reporting them at management level for immediate handling and investigation.
  • Regularly revising and redefining the policy and procedures, taking into account needs and developments in workplaces.

6. Personnel information and awareness-raising actions

THEMELI S.A. declares its zero tolerance of phenomena of violence and harassment and undertakes the following actions to raise its personnel’s awareness of these phenomena:

  • Informing personnel and raising their awareness
  • Posting the relevant procedures in a conspicuous place and in an online space accessible to all employees
  • On-site training at construction sites and workplaces outside the company’s head offices

7. Information on the rights and obligations of employees and of THEMELI S.A. in the event of the occurrence, reporting or complaint of such incidents, and on the relevant procedure.

The company undertakes to provide information on the rights and obligations of employees and of the employer, as well as of persons exercising managerial authority or representing the employer, to the extent and degree of their own responsibility, in the event of the occurrence, reporting or complaint of such incidents, and on the relevant procedure for receiving and examining complaints.

Every employee is entitled:

  • To be treated with courtesy and respect
  • Not to be the recipient of violent conduct
  • To have the right to complain of any incident of prohibited conduct

Every employee is obliged:

  • To keep informed about matters of combating incidents of violence and harassment at work
  • To report any incident of violence and harassment to the company’s Responsible Officer
  • To cooperate in the event of an investigation of an incident
  • To comply with the company’s Policy

In addition, any person affected by an incident of violence or harassment during access to employment, during the employment relationship, or even if the contract or employment relationship in the context of which the incident or conduct allegedly occurred has ended, has the following options:

  • filing a complaint within the business, in accordance with the complaints management policy.
  • filing a report with the Greek Ombudsman within the scope of its statutory competences,
  • filing a complaint with the Labour Inspectorate within the scope of its statutory competences, and
  • recourse to judicial protection,

The contact details of the competent authorities are as follows:

  • Greek Ombudsman line: 213 13 06 600 (e-mail: press@sinigoros.gr),
  • Labour Inspectorate line via the citizen service line 1555 (e-mail: helpdesk@sepenet.gr),
  • SOS line 15900 for immediate psychological support and counselling for women victims of gender-based violence.

8. Appointment of a contact person (“liaison”) responsible for guiding and informing employees on the prevention and combating of violence and harassment.

In the first instance, every employee is given the opportunity to request the intervention of our company following an incident of violence or harassment.

The Company ensures employees’ easy and direct access to the contact person, and for this purpose the means of communication provided for include both in-person contact and contact by email, telephone, etc.

The personnel department informs employees about the prevention and combating of violence and harassment at work, as well as about the responsible persons they may approach for the receipt, examination and handling of complaints or grievances.

9. Protection of the employment and support of employees who are victims of domestic violence.

The Company takes, to the extent possible, measures to protect the employment of, and to support, employees who are victims of domestic violence.

Any employee who has suffered domestic violence with repercussions also in the workplace may report this fact orally or in writing to their supervisor or to the Compliance Officer, so that the Company is informed and can take the appropriate and necessary measures, such as, for example, informing and cooperating with the competent authorities

11. Investigation and examination of complaints with impartiality and protection of the confidentiality and personal data of victims and of the persons complained against

The receipt, investigation and handling of complaints is carried out promptly and impartially, in a manner that respects human dignity and ensures the protection of the confidentiality and of the personal data of victims and of the persons complained against, which are collected in the exercise of the above duties.

It is pointed out that the company’s legal representative and the competent contact persons, as well as any services involved, undertake to receive and in no way to obstruct the receipt of relevant complaints, to investigate and handle every relevant complaint promptly, to investigate and examine the matters complained of impartially, with discretion and with respect for human dignity, and to take immediate measures to protect the affected person. In this context:

  • Persons who submit a complaint will be informed of the receipt of the complaint and, at regular intervals, of the progress of the examination of their complaint.
  • If possible, an effort will be made to resolve the incident in a conciliatory manner before it escalates.
  • To safeguard workplace peace and the integrity of the investigations, the Management of THEMELI S.A. may, among other things, relocate the affected person or the person complained against, or modify their working hours or the place or manner in which their work is performed, or even terminate the employment or cooperation relationship, subject to the prohibition of abuse of right under Article 281 of the Civil Code
  • Before taking any measure, the Management of THEMELI S.A. calls the person complained against to present a defence, unless it judges on reasoned grounds that this is not required, either because it would endanger the affected person, or because there is irrefutable evidence as to whether or not the act constituting an incident of violence and harassment was committed, or for any other reason.
  • If the incident also constitutes an unlawful act and entails criminal or administrative sanctions, the Management of THEMELI S.A. cooperates with the competent authorities (indicatively and without limitation, judicial and prosecutorial authorities, the Labour Inspectorate (SEPE), the Greek Ombudsman, etc.).
  • The Management of THEMELI S.A. and the “Liaison” undertake to maintain confidentiality and to protect the Personal Data collected in the exercise of the above duties.

12. Prohibition of retaliation and further victimisation of the affected person.

The company undertakes that it observes the obligation to prohibit retaliation against affected persons, in accordance with Article 13 of Law 4808/20215 and the provisions of labour legislation.

The termination or dissolution in any manner of the legal relationship on which the employment is based, as well as any other unfavourable treatment of an affected person, is prohibited and void where it constitutes retaliatory conduct or a countermeasure against that person by reason of the submission of a complaint of an incident of violence and harassment

For the Termination of Cooperation with complainants following a complaint, and in order to avoid retaliatory conduct or countermeasures, the competent body for taking the decision is the company’s Board of Directors, after examining the views of both the complainant and the person complained against.

13. Description of the consequences upon a finding of violations of this policy

Where a breach of the prohibition of violence and harassment under Article 4 of Law 4808/2021 is found, the company takes the measures necessary, appropriate and proportionate in each case, in order to prevent and avoid the repetition of a similar incident or conduct. These measures may include recommendations, their repetition and the pointing out of the consequences, the separation of the person complained against and the complainant (if feasible, by a change of job position, duties, workplace, etc.), protection of the complainant from other colleagues, and a corresponding admonition of the person complained against.

Finally, if the problem is not remedied, the company will proceed to terminate the employment contract of the person complained against and/or of the complainant (taking into account the weight, the correctness and the seriousness of the complaint, as well as its capabilities and its corporate needs).

14. Cooperation with and provision of all relevant information to the competent authorities, if requested.

Cooperation with and the provision of information to the competent authorities is critical to ensuring the investigation of complaints and the taking of appropriate measures to protect employees and address violations.

The company and every person or service competent for the receipt and handling of complaints of violence and harassment must cooperate harmoniously and will provide their cooperation and assistance, as well as all relevant information, to the competent public, administrative or judicial authority which, either of its own motion or following a request submitted by an affected person, within the scope of its competence, requests the provision of data or information, and undertakes for this purpose to provide assistance and access to the data as laid down in Article 5(1) of Law 4808/2021.

For this purpose, any data gathered, in any form, are kept in a relevant file in compliance with the provisions of Law 4624/20196 and are delivered without delay to the competent authorities when this is deemed necessary.

15. Notification

This policy is notified to all employees of THEMELI S.A. by any appropriate means (including, and deemed sufficient, its sending by email to their corporate address or to another personal address if they do not have a corporate one) and is posted at the registered office, the site offices and the website of the company.

It is also countersigned, in confirmation of being informed of the content of the policy and of accepting it, by every person hired after the date on which it was drawn up and by every existing employee upon the first renewal of their contract (if such renewal is provided for).
