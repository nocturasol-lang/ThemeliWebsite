BUSINESS CONTINUITY POLICY

The objective of THEMELI S.A. is to ensure the continued operation of the company’s critical activities and their effective recovery following a disruptive incident or a natural disaster.

The company has developed and implements an integrated Business Continuity Management System in accordance with the requirements of the international standard ISO 22301:2019.

Within the framework of this System, continuous and systematic identification of risks and threats and assessment of their impacts is carried out, so that an effective response and recovery can be planned and tested in advance.

To achieve this, the following have been developed:

  • Business Impact Analysis Study
  • Risk Assessment
  • Business Continuity Plans
  • Business Continuity Procedures,

which cover all of the company’s critical activities and are reviewed and updated at regular intervals so that their content accurately reflects the current situation.

The Business Continuity Plans are tested at regular intervals through preparedness exercises, to ensure that they will be activated successfully in emergency situations and that the Management and employees understand how to implement them, their roles and their responsibilities.

The Management is committed to the continual improvement of the Business Continuity Management System by providing the necessary resources, setting as its priority in the event of disruptive incidents:

  • the safety of employees and facilities
  • the continued operation of critical activities
  • the restoration of operation within the set time limits
