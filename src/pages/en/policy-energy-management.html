ENERGY MANAGEMENT POLICY

The Management of THEMELI S.A. is committed to the responsible management of energy as part of its overall Environmental Policy, across all of its facilities.

THEMELI S.A. seeks to rationalise its use of energy by minimising

  • energy costs.
  • the environmental impacts of harmful emissions.
  • the depletion of fossil fuels.

while maintaining its business objectives as well as an acceptable working environment for its employees.

The Management provides the necessary resources so that the following strategic aims are achieved:

  • using energy as efficiently as possible.
  • developing a system for target-setting and performance monitoring.
  • investing in energy-efficient facilities and projects with a short payback period.
  • the energy footprint is the guiding criterion for design, refurbishments and equipment.
  • publishing specific energy consumption targets on an annual basis, with a related progress report.
  • minimising gas emissions (CO2) and pollutants likely to cause damage to the environment.
  • procuring goods and services from organisations that demonstrate a positive commitment to energy efficiency, where this is practically and financially feasible.
  • cultivating personnel’s awareness of energy use matters, so that they too are able to propose saving measures and apply them in their daily routine.
  • complying with applicable legal requirements and other regulatory requirements relating to energy use, consumption and performance.

To fulfil all of the above, and within the framework of the Environmental Management system it applies, the Management of THEMELI S.A. is putting into effect a specialised Energy Management System in accordance with the standard ISO 50001:2018, so as to ensure the correct implementation of this policy and the continual improvement of energy performance.
