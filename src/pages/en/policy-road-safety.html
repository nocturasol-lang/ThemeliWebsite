COMPANY POLICY ON ROAD SAFETY

The objective of THEMELI S.A. is to be a commercial, profitable organisation, aspiring to establish a long and sustainable relationship with our clients, constantly striving to minimise the impacts of our activities on the environment and ensuring the health, safety and well-being of our employees and partners, always committed to compliance with the law.

Our success in delivering our products and services will be assessed both by the client’s opinion and by our own, while the effort to readjust and improve our own performance so that it meets the client’s requirements will be continuous. The philosophy behind the delivery of our products and services is achieved through the following:

  • Adopting a partnership-type approach with clients, encouraging a mutually beneficial feedback process.
  • Developing a direct and flexible response in our communication with our clients and recognising the various needs of our clients’ organisations.
  • Investing in our personnel at all levels through careful and rational training, while recognising the essential role that the know-how, experience and dedication of the personnel play in the success of our organisation.
  • Developing all those appropriate operational procedures for achieving the desired objective
  • Constantly striving to deliver to the client the best possible value for the products we provide, which is assessed as the comparison between the cost of the project to the client and the benefit the client expects from it.
  • Continually seeking and adopting methods for improving safety and occupational health in our working environment.

Our objectives are implemented through our Company’s Integrated Management System, which conforms to the requirements of the standard ISO 39001:2012.

The procedures are defined in the Operations Manual, in which they are also documented so as to conform to the provisions of all the aforementioned standards, ensuring that the efficient and effective Quality Management, Occupational Health and Safety Management and Environmental Management in all areas and activities of the Company are ongoing and continually improving.

Compliance with the provisions of this document is mandatory and binding for all personnel and partners of our company. This document is available to all personnel of our Company.

The achievement of high Quality, the consolidation of Environmental Awareness and the safeguarding of Occupational Health and Safety are the responsibility of everyone in the business, and the continual improvement of Products and Services, as well as of the image of the business, is the result of the daily actions of each member of THEMELI S.A.: Management, Personnel and Partners.
