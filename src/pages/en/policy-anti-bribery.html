COMPANY POLICY AGAINST BRIBERY

The objective of THEMELI S.A., recognising the importance of combating corruption and bribery, is to establish a long and sustainable relationship with its employees, clients and partners.

The philosophy behind the delivery of our services is achieved through the following:

  • Full compliance with the applicable legal framework for combating bribery.
  • Investing in our personnel at all levels through careful and rational training, while recognising the essential role that the know-how, experience and dedication of the personnel play in the success of our organisation.
  • Developing all those appropriate operational procedures for achieving the desired anti-bribery objective.
  • Constantly striving to deliver to the client the best possible value for the products we provide, which is assessed as the comparison between the cost of the project to the client and the benefit the client expects from it.
  • Continually seeking and adopting methods for improving the anti-bribery management system.

Our objectives are implemented through our Company’s Integrated Anti-Bribery Management System, which conforms to the requirements of the standard ISO 37001:2016.

The procedures are defined in the Anti-Bribery System Management Manual, in which they are also documented so as to conform to the provisions of the aforementioned standard, ensuring that the efficient and effective Management of Bribery in all areas and activities of the Company is ongoing and continually improving.

Compliance with the provisions of this document is mandatory and binding for all personnel and partners of our company. This document is available to all personnel, clients and partners of our Company.

Achieving the elimination of instances of bribery is the responsibility of everyone in the business and, together with the image of the business, is the result of the daily actions of each member of THEMELI S.A.: Management, Personnel and Partners.
