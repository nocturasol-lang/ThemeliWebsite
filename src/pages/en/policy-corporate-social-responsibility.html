COMPANY POLICY ON CORPORATE SOCIAL RESPONSIBILITY

THEMELI S.A., recognising its social and economic sensitivity towards its employees and society at large, adopts this Policy, through which it aims to manage with corporate social responsibility matters relating to:

  • forced labour
  • health and safety
  • free participation in professional associations
  • discrimination and disciplinary practices
  • working hours and staff remuneration

By satisfying the above conditions, THEMELI S.A. aims to develop and maintain a working environment based on Corporate Social Responsibility.

To achieve the above objectives, THEMELI S.A. commits to the following:

  • Its compliance with the legislative requirements in force from time to time.
  • The development, installation and implementation of an integrated Corporate Social Responsibility System in accordance with the standard ISO 26000:2010.
  • The appropriate and adequate training/briefing of its personnel on social responsibility matters, through specialised seminars and appropriate work instructions/procedures.
  • The continuous monitoring, documentation and evaluation of the conditions of social and workplace harmony and responsibility, as well as the review of this Policy with a view to continual improvement.
  • The allocation of the necessary resources to fulfil the above objectives.

THEMELI S.A. takes it as given that each of its employees will make every reasonably possible effort to fulfil their own responsibilities and obligations, responding to the Company’s commitment and thereby contributing to the implementation of this Policy. The same applies to every contractor, supplier or other interested third party of THEMELI S.A.
